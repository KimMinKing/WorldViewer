Unicode data
------------

The offline pinyin table is derived from Unicode 17.0.0 Unihan kMandarin.
It is distributed under the Unicode License v3. The full license is included
as unicode_license_v3.txt in this directory. Exact source and generated hashes

Source: https://www.unicode.org/Public/17.0.0/ucd/Unihan.zip

CC-CEDICT dictionary data
-------------------------

The packaged exact-match Chinese dictionary index is generated from
CC-CEDICT release 2026-09-26T00:33:45Z, published by MDBG and community
contributors. It contains traditional and simplified headwords, numbered
pinyin and English definitions. CC-CEDICT and WorldViewer's generated index
are distributed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International. A copy is included as cc_by_sa_4.0.txt. Exact source and
generated hashes are recorded in ../dictionary/cc_cedict_20260926.source.txt.

Source: https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cc-cedict
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Google ML Kit on-device Translation
-----------------------------------

WorldViewer uses com.google.mlkit:translate:17.0.3 to translate CC-CEDICT's
English definitions to Korean on the device after the user explicitly asks
for a Korean dictionary result. The SDK can download and update translation
models and send performance and utilization metrics to Google. The selected
Chinese text and translation input/output are processed on-device according
to Google's ML Kit privacy documentation.

SDK terms and privacy: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms
Translation usage guidelines: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/language/translation/translation-terms
Attribution requirements: https://cloud.google.com/translate/attribution

The "powered by Google Translate" badge was obtained from the official
attribution asset package and is displayed adjacent to translated results.
It remains a Google brand feature governed by Google's attribution and brand
guidelines; its inclusion does not imply endorsement of WorldViewer.
